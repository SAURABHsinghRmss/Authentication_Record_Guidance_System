# Student Role (student.txt) — Minute Functional Specifications & Roadmap

> Detailed technical breakdown of every function, route, database mechanism, and UI feature associated with the **Student** (`student`) role in EduIdentityAI.

---

## 1. Unified 3-Category Chat System (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Omnichannel communication partitioned across 3 distinct security envelopes:
  * **PERSONAL (1-on-1 Direct)**: Direct messaging with faculty mentors, parents, and the EduMentor Meta AI copilot.
  * **PRIVATE (Enclosed Clubs & Study Groups)**: Collaborative discussion in restricted student clubs, project teams, and hackathon cohorts.
  * **GLOBAL (Campus Agora)**: Campus-wide broadcast agora, official institutional notices, and cross-department open discussions.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/chat/categories`: Returns metadata, active channels, and unread message counters.
  * `GET /api/chat/category/{category}`: Loads chronological messages filtered by category and channel/peer.
  * `POST /api/chat/send`: Dispatches new messages and cross-syncs with legacy tables.
* **Frontend UI**: Integrated Unified Campus Agora & Chat Room (`unified_chat` tab) with category pill switcher.

## 2. EduMentor Meta AI in WhatsApp-Style Chat (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: 24/7 educational AI copilot grounded in **8 Core Educational Criteria**:
  1. `POLICY_REGULATION`: Institutional attendance requirements (75% threshold) and credit transfers (20% SWAYAM/NPTEL).
  2. `CONCEPT_TUTORING`: Step-by-step concept tutoring, analogies, and Big-O complexity analysis.
  3. `QUIZ_GENERATION`: Interactive 4-option practice MCQs with hints and expandable answer/explanation reveals.
  4. `THREAD_SUMMARIZATION`: Synthesis of study group messages and lecture threads into structured study notes.
  5. `ACADEMIC_INTEGRITY`: IEEE and APA 7th edition reference formatting and ethical AI research conduct.
  6. `CAREER_PATHWAY`: Semester milestones, placement preparation, and technical interview roadmaps.
  7. `EXAM_REVISION`: High-yield formula cheat sheets, common exam traps, and probable viva questions.
  8. `CODE_LAB_ASSIST`: Line-by-line algorithmic walkthroughs, debugging hints, and complexity optimization.
* **Invocation Mechanisms**:
  * **Dedicated 1-on-1 AI Contact**: Click `@edumentor_ai` with the glowing iridescent Meta AI ring avatar.
  * **In-Channel Mentions**: Tag `@ai` or `@meta` in any Global Agora or Private Club conversation.
  * **Criteria Quick-Action Bar**: Tap any of the 8 criteria pill chips above the input bar.
* **Dual-Engine Architecture**: Powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash (`models/gemini-3.8-flash:generateContent`) with zero-downtime 3s fallback to the local RAG vector store.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/chat/ai/criteria`: Returns complete criteria catalog with sample prompts and color badges.
  * `POST /api/chat/ai/ask`: Standalone endpoint for direct educational queries.

## 3. Academic Records, RAG Guidance & DigiLocker Auto-Fill (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Accessing personal academic transcript metrics (CGPA, attendance, credits, backlogs) and auto-populating registration details from DigiLocker.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/students/{id}`
  * `POST /rag_query`: Semantic vector similarity search over academic regulations.
  * `POST /api/auth/digilocker-auto-fill`: Extracts certified name, roll number, department, and semester from verified credentials.
* **Frontend UI**: Academic Guidance Advisor tab and DigiLocker Permission Modal.

## 4. Multimodal Biometrics & Multi-Factor Authentication (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Multi-layered biometric verification via Face, Voice, OCR, and Multimodal Fusion alongside 6-digit Email and Mobile OTP passcodes.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `POST /api/auth/biometric-verify`
  * `POST /api/auth/otp/send`, `POST /api/auth/otp/verify`
  * `POST /api/auth/otp/mobile/send`, `POST /api/auth/otp/mobile/verify`
* **Frontend UI**: Biometrics & 2FA verification card grid.

## 5. Opportunity Scam Shield & URL Verifier (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Scanning external scholarship, internship, and competition URLs to protect students from academic phishing and financial aid scams.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `POST /api/security/check-url`: Analyzes URL heuristics, TLD risks, and calculates domain trust scores.
* **Frontend UI**: Opportunity Scam Shield tab with risk gauge and AI safety advice.

## 6. Career Pathways & Placement Intelligence (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Tracking career competencies, placement drive readiness, and technical skills portfolio connected to `databases/career_pathways.db`.
* **Frontend UI**: Integrated career profile summary and placement cell timeline.
