# Institute / Institution Role (institute.txt) — Minute Functional Specifications

> Detailed technical breakdown of every function, policy, and compliance workflow associated with the **Institute** (`institute`) role in EduIdentityAI.

---

## 1. Institutional Governance & Tenant Oversight
* **Function Purpose**: Oversight of institutional departments, affiliated faculty, and enrolled student bodies.
* **API Integration**: Connects with institutional authentication middleware to verify that user email domains match authorized college registries (e.g., `@uni.edu`).
* **Compliance & Accreditation**: Ensures institutional data integrity against national academic depository standards (NAD / APAAR ID registry).

## 2. Institution Registration & Status Management
* **Function Purpose**: Submitting institutional accreditation details during organization onboarding.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `POST /api/auth/register`: Submits organization name, admin email, and credentials.
  * Status Tracking: Monitors whether the institution status is `PENDING_REVIEW` or `VERIFIED` by system administrators.
