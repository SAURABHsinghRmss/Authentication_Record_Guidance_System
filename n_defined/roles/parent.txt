# Parent / Guardian Role (parent.txt) — Minute Functional Specifications

> Detailed technical breakdown of every function, monitoring view, and alert mechanism associated with the **Parent / Guardian** (`parent`) role in EduIdentityAI.

---

## 1. Ward Academic & Attendance Monitoring (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Guardian oversight of linked student's semester progress, cumulative CGPA, credits earned, and attendance percentage compliance against the mandatory 75% threshold.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/students/{id}/guidance`: Retrieves automated AI guidance reports and attendance compliance status against the mandatory 75% threshold.
* **Frontend UI**: Parent Overview dashboard featuring ward profile card, attendance metric rings, and backlog warning banners.

## 2. Direct 1-on-1 Faculty Mentor Consultation (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Confidential, direct two-way messaging between parents/guardians and their ward's assigned faculty mentor.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/chat/category/PERSONAL?peer_username=faculty`: Retrieves personal advisory thread.
  * `POST /api/chat/send`: Dispatches confidential parental inquiries (`sender_role: "parent"`).
* **Frontend UI**: Direct Message Ward's Faculty Mentor interface integrated into the 3-Category Unified Chat window.

## 3. EduMentor Meta AI Educational Advisory for Parents (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Providing parents with clear, jargon-free explanations of statutory academic frameworks:
  * Using **`POLICY_REGULATION`** to verify credit transfer policies (SWAYAM/NPTEL) and exam eligibility requirements.
  * Using **`CAREER_PATHWAY`** to understand internship milestones, campus placement schedules, and technical career progression.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/chat/ai/criteria`
  * `POST /api/chat/ai/ask`
* **Frontend UI**: Dedicated Meta AI chat with iridescent ring avatar and quick-action prompt cards.

## 4. Ward Safety & Opportunity Scam Shield (Implemented)
* **Function Purpose**: Protecting students and families against predatory scholarship scams, fraudulent tuition fee links, and fake internship offers.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `POST /api/security/check-url`: Verifies institutional validity and safety score of external links.
* **Frontend UI**: Opportunity Scam Shield tab with risk score gauges and proactive defense advice.
