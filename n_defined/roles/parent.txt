# Parent / Guardian Role (parent.txt) — Minute Functional Specifications

> Detailed technical breakdown of every function, monitoring view, and alert mechanism associated with the **Parent / Guardian** (`parent`) role in EduIdentityAI.

---

## 1. Student Academic & Attendance Monitoring
* **Function Purpose**: Guardian oversight of student semester progress, cumulative CGPA, credits earned, and attendance percentage.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/students/{id}/guidance`: Retrieves automated AI guidance reports and attendance compliance status against the mandatory 75% threshold.

## 2. Safety & Opportunity Scam Shield Alerts
* **Function Purpose**: Protecting students and guardians against predatory scholarship scams, fake internships, and fraudulent fee payment portals.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `POST /api/security/check-url`: Verifies institutional validity of links accessed by the student.
