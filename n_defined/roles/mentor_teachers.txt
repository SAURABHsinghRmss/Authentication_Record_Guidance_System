# Mentor Teachers / Faculty Role (mentor_teachers.txt) — Minute Functional Specifications

> Detailed technical breakdown of every function, route, and UI feature associated with the **Mentor Teachers / Faculty** (`faculty`) role in EduIdentityAI.

---

## 1. Mentorship Roster Management
* **Function Purpose**: Viewing and filtering assigned student mentees across academic departments, semesters, CGPA, and attendance metrics.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/students`: Retrieves all student records for faculty review.
* **Frontend UI**: Mentorship Roster tabular view (`faculty_roster` tab) with search and sorting.

## 2. At-Risk Student Early Warning & Counseling Notices
* **Function Purpose**: Identifying students falling below the mandatory 75% attendance threshold or carrying active backlogs, and dispatching remediation alerts.
* **Algorithmic Threshold**: `parseFloat(student.attendance_percentage) < 75 || student.backlogs > 0`.
* **Frontend UI**: At-Risk Students tab (`faculty_at_risk`) with one-click **"Send Counseling Notice"** button generating automated remediation logs.

## 3. Mentor-Student Direct Chat System
* **Function Purpose**: Secure, real-time messaging between specific faculty mentors and their assigned student mentees.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `GET /api/chat/messages/{student_id}?mentor_username=faculty`: Loads chronological conversation history.
  * `POST /api/chat/messages`: Sends a direct message with `sender_role: "faculty"`.
* **Persistence**: Stored in the `mentor_chats` relational table.
* **Frontend UI**: Faculty Mentorship Chat tab with dropdown selector for assigned student mentees, auto-updating chat bubbles, and input controls.

## 4. Research & Conference Portal Verifier
* **Function Purpose**: Validating academic journals, conference submission websites, and grant portals before faculty recommend them to mentees.
* **API Endpoints**:
  * `POST /api/security/check-url`: Evaluates domain heuristic trust scores.
* **Frontend UI**: Research Portal Verifier tab (`legitimacy`) with faculty advisory guidance.
